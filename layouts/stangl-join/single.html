Postdocs

We do not have openings for postdocs at this time. If you are interested in writing a joint proposal to apply for funding through a fellowship (such as the BU Center for Systems Neuroscience Postdoc Fellowship) or other internal/external funding mechanisms, please reach out to: mstangl@bu.edu

Prospective PhD students

There are several pathways to joining the lab as a PhD student, including the PhD Program in Biomedical Engineering (BME), the PhD Program in Brain, Behavior, and Cognition (BBC), and the Graduate Program in Neuroscience (GPN). Please find more information about these programs at their respective website.

Admitted PhD students at Boston University

If you would like to do a lab rotation or join the lab as a PhD student, and you have already been admitted to a PhD program at Boston University, please reach out to: mstangl@bu.edu

Undergraduate and Master's students

If you are interested in cognitive neuroscience, human brain imaging methods, and data analysis, we would love to hear from you and explore options. Please reach out to: mstangl@bu.edu

VR/AR game designers and software engineers

We are looking for talented and motivated virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) game designers and software engineers. If you are interested in developing virtual environments and objects for cognitive neuroscience studies, please reach out to: mstangl@bu.edu
